Changelog v1.1.0
Version History & Release Notes
v1.1.0 — Inverter Settings & Dual-Slot Scheduling 6 Nov 2026
  • Dedicated Settings View: Interactive controls for FoxESS inverter holding registers (FC06/FC16) under a dedicated sidebar link.
  • Operating Work Mode Selector: Switch between Self-Use, Feed-in Priority, Backup, Force Charge, and Force Discharge.
  • Dual-Period Force Charge Schedule: Independent control for Slot 1 and Slot 2 with a one-click ⚡ Align with Tariff shortcut.
  • Battery Safety Reserves & Current Rate: Live configuration of Min SoC reserve (10%–30%) and maximum charge/discharge current limits.
v1.0.0 — Initial Open-Source Release 6 Nov 2026
  • Zero-Cloud Local Inverter Control: Modbus RTU-over-TCP support for FoxESS systems with live power flow animation.
  • Auto-Discovery & Setup Wizard: 4-step first-time onboarding with subnet scan for inverters, Hue bridges & Shelly plugs.
  • Multi-Supplier Energy Tariffs: Full support for Octopus (Go, Intelligent, Agile, Cosy, Tracker), British Gas, E.ON Next, OVO, EDF, Scottish Power, and Ofgem Price Cap.
  • 48-Slot Agile Rates Curve & Planner: Real-time half-hourly wholesale export tracking and discharge recommendations.
  • Smart Battery Bridging: ML-powered countdown and confidence meter predicting battery endurance to the 00:30 off-peak window.
  • Hardware Plugs & Smart Lighting: TP-Link Tapo (KLAP v2), Shelly (local HTTP RPC), and Philips Hue integration.
